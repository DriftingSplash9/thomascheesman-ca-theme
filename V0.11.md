## Known sharp edges

1. **Video playback cannot be verified in an automated/MCP browser.**
   Every video tested (the `.mov`, both `.mp4` pairs, even a tiny
   embedded test clip) showed `readyState: 0` in the agent's controlled
   Chrome — H.264 decoding does not work in that environment. **Do not
   re-diagnose the crest video by reading `readyState`.** Verify video
   files by byte inspection instead (moov-atom position, codec fourcc),
   or trust Thomas's own-browser eyeball. Thomas confirmed the crest
   videos played fine in his real browser.

2. **Crest video files are large** (~62 MB each). Web-optimized so they
   *start* fast, but heavy bandwidth for a hidden easter egg. Optional
   future optimization — re-encode at 720p / lower bitrate. Not a
   blocker.

3. **Astra parent `style.css` returned a one-off 503** during smoke
   testing. The child theme overrides everything visible so the page
   rendered fine. Re-check on a couple of home-page loads — if it
   persists (vs. a transient Hostinger LiteSpeed hiccup), investigate.

4. **/contact email obfuscation is rot13 + JS-decode only.** Modern
   scrapers run JS, so the address is effectively exposed. "Good
   enough", not real protection. No action unless Thomas wants more.

5. **Leaderboards are open-write** (carried from V0.10). Anyone with
   dev tools can POST a score. Acceptable per Thomas.

6. **Photo-gallery counts in `inc/desk-menu.php`** are hardcoded — bump
   them if gallery rows change (carried from V0.10).

7. **Untracked dev helpers at repo root** — `build-image-audit.py`,
   `test-image-urls.py`, `image-audit*.xlsx`, `remove-dead-images.py`.
   Delete after go-live or add to `.gitignore` (carried from V0.10).
