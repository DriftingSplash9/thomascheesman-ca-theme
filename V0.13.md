## Known sharp edges

1. **Hostinger LiteSpeed caching.** After every push + auto-deploy,
   pages can serve stale HTML/assets until a manual **Purge All** in
   Hostinger / LiteSpeed Cache. This bit nearly every deploy this
   session. After any future deploy, expect to purge.
2. **Dormant-by-design files.** `single.php`,
   `single-post-make-canada-great-again.php`, `page-journal.php` and
   `tc_render_read_next()` are all inert with no published posts —
   kept for draft preview and in case posts ever return. The old
   `.blog-section` / `.post-card` CSS in style.css is now unused —
   optional cleanup someday, harmless as-is.
3. **Posts are Drafts, not deleted** — fully recoverable. If a post is
   re-published, its template lights back up automatically.
4. **Video playback can't be verified in an automated browser** —
   verify the crest "secret videos" and Daniel's crawl clip in a real
   browser.
5. **Photo-gallery lazy-load quirk** (carried from V0.12) — some
   masonry images may stay blank until hovered; verify on the kid
   pages in a real browser.
6. The desk menu's top-right **MENU trigger** sometimes needs two
   clicks in an automated browser — a tooling artifact, fine for real
   visitors.
7. The **diary CSV** (`C:\Users\thoma\Documents\Claude-Diary\diary.csv`
   per memory) was **not reachable** from the tools this session — the
   `Documents` path returned "not found". No diary row was appended.
