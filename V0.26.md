## The systems a new agent must know (carried from V0.25, still exact)

1. **Heritage long-reads:** `{Line}-Story-EDITED.md` (Rycroft & Steinke are `-DRAFT.md`) in `Heritage research\` → `node _md2heritage.js <md> <out.php> "<Label>" <json>` → `inc/heritage/{line}-story-body.php`. Labels: Lakeman=`"Lakeman"`, Verboom=`"Verboom"`, rest `"The X"`. The `{line}-images.json` keys kickers/pullquotes/figures/ribbons/`tree`/`video` **to the literal chapter-heading text** — a chapter merge or rename without the matching json re-key silently drops that chapter's chrome. Single-`##` sections (the Verboom appendix now) key by the full heading text (eyebrow is empty; converter falls back to title lookup). Verify regens with grep, **not** `git diff --stat`: the bodies are a handful of very long lines, so even a big prose change shows as ~1 changed line per file.
