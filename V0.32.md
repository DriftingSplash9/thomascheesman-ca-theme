### 3. CSP `'unsafe-eval'` — diagnosed + fixed the blank Media Library
Thomas's wp-admin Media Library was rendering **blank**. Root cause (verified): the `.htaccess` CSP applies to `/wp-admin/` too, and a prior session (G9) had dropped `'unsafe-eval'` — which WP admin's underscore/Backbone templates (`wp.template()` → `new Function()`) **require**. Thomas re-added `'unsafe-eval'` to the `.htaccess` `script-src`; verified live, admin restored. **Lesson:** never drop `'unsafe-eval'` site-wide on WordPress without exempting wp-admin. (This is why V0.31's **SEC-2** is correctly scoped to *front-end only*.) [[project_tc_csp_htaccess_authoritative]].

## ⭐ The COMMENTS feature — scoped with Thomas this thread, NOT yet built

This was the feature Thomas picked to do next; we agreed to build the **family login first** (done) because the private tier depends on it. The design we settled on (his decisions):

- **Two tiers:**
  - **Open + moderated** on Thomas's own pages: **HCS, About, Thomasito (/family/thomas), the family hub (/family), and the 8 heritage LONG-READS** — *long-reads only* (his explicit choice: NOT the short spokes or the hub).
  - **Private (family-login-gated)** on the **kids' pages** (Patience/Daniel/Faith). Now even simpler since the audit thread gated those whole pages — comments there are inherently family-only.
- **Behaviour:** native WP **threaded** comments (comment-on-comment, ~5 deep); **every comment held for manual approval** (nothing public until Thomas approves; he's emailed via the Gmail SMTP shim); **honeypot** anti-spam (no plugin, stays at 5 plugins); **name + email required** (email never shown); **no external Gravatars** (use a coloured monogram — keeps the no-tracking promise); guests may comment on the open tier (no login).
- **Build sketch:** `comments.php` + `inc/comments.php` (force `comments_open` on the target slugs, force threading, honeypot + auto-moderation, styled list callback, conditional asset enqueue) + `assets/css/comments.css` + `comments_template()` calls in the long-read chrome and the his-pages templates. Page-tier helper keyed by slug (heritage long-read = slug `story` + orphans `mcivers`/`verbooms`/`steinkes`; his pages = `hcs`/`about`/`thomas`/`family`). Remember to also block REST/feeds exposure of comments on the gated kid pages.

This is the natural next feature now the login exists — **weigh it against V0.31's audit Short-Term tier (IA-1 etc.) with Thomas.**
