## ⚠️ Read first — the two open issues Thomas flagged at wrap

1. **Building scale vs the buggy (3D).** The farm buildings (farmhouse,
   church, barn, etc.) read **too small** next to the buggy. The treehouses
   were already scaled 1.75× (1.0.682) — the rest of the buildings need the
   same treatment. Options: scale each build-function's dimensions, or apply
   `g.scale.set(~1.6)` per landmark in `raiseLandmark()` + enlarge the Matter
   footprints (`lm.w/lm.h`) to match. **Do the physics footprints too** or
   the buggy will drive through walls it can see.
2. **Jumps too small — "not catching much air."** The three MOUNDS
   (a 18–22, r 54–66) launch weakly. Fixes to try together: taller/steeper
   mounds (a 30–40), stronger crest launch (`-groundRate * 0.9` → 1.2, cap
   150 → 220), and/or lower gravity while boosted. Space bunny-hop (95+sp*5)
   could also scale up. Thomas wants **monster-truck air**.

### 🧭 3D state reference (read before touching)
- **One file:** `assets/js/back-quarter-3d.js` (~1,300 lines). All world data
  at the top: `LANDMARKS`, `LINES` (drive-ins), `PATHS`, `HILLS` (incl. pond
  bowls as negative gaussians), `FLAT` (yard flatteners — **add one whenever
  you add a building**), `MOUNDS`, `PADS`, `TOKENS`, `PONDS`, `COMPOUND`.
- Terrain = `rawHills` (tilt + gaussians) → `hillsAt` (FLAT blending) →
  `heightAt` (+ mounds). Ground mesh bakes roads via vertex colors — **road
  changes require editing PATHS *and* nothing else** (painting recomputes at
  boot).
- Render interpolates between 60Hz physics steps (`pX/pY/pA` + alpha) —
  don't bypass it or the jitter returns.
- Buggy rotation order **YZX** (yaw + `airPitch` for flips).
- ⭐ Verification limit: automation tabs freeze rAF/WebGL — verify *loads
  clean*; Thomas drives.
