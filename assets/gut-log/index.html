How to use

  1. Choose your meal pattern each day.
  2. Record foods simply — quantities and textures matter more than perfect calories.
  3. Check off every supplement and medication dose taken. Note time where useful.
  4. For bowel movements: time + Bristol type (1–7) + intensity. No blood or mucus expected.
  5. Rate appetite, pain, sleep, energy, and skin. Record weight at bedtime.

Bristol Stool Scale

1 — Separate hard lumps (nuts), hard to pass

2 — Sausage-shaped but lumpy

3 — Sausage with cracks on surface

4 — Smooth soft sausage/snake — ideal

5 — Soft blobs with clear edges

6 — Fluffy pieces, ragged edges — mushy

7 — Watery, no solid pieces — liquid
