# The Back Quarter — P1 world-painting prompts (for Grok Imagine)

> Feeds P1 of `QUARTER-SECTION-SPEC.md`. The painting is the **static
> terrain + buildings only**. All moving/glowing/interactive bits (buggy,
> mailbox flag, chimney smoke, neon, lantern glows, blinking mast,
> billboards, labels) are drawn procedurally in Pixi *on top* — so the
> painting must NOT include text, labels, UI, people, or vehicles.
>
> Deliver a still image; grade/crop in CapCut. Ratio **4:3 preferred**
> (3:2 or 16:9 also fine — Claude adapts the world box to whatever lands).
> Highest resolution available; aim ~2600×1950+.

---

## PRIMARY PROMPT (paste this)

A top-down high-oblique illustrated map of a single prairie quarter-section
farm at dusk, Peace Country Alberta, in the style of a cozy hand-painted
video-game overworld (Final Fantasy VII world map, Zelda: A Link to the Past,
Stardew Valley). A bird's-eye view tilted slightly so rooftops and treetops
have gentle depth. Rutted dirt wagon-paths wind across the land connecting a
scatter of buildings: a small farmhouse with a warm yellow-lit window, a
little roadside cookshack/diner, a tall wooden grain elevator, a weathered red
barn, a white country church on a low grassy rise, a tight windbreak row of
poplar trees sheltering a couple of small treehouses, a slim radio tower on
the far edge, a lonely little shed tucked off the paths, and a farm gate with a
mailbox at the bottom-centre edge. Stubble fields and mowed hay strips in muted
greens and golds, wire fences with posts around the border, a few ponds
catching the last light. Soft nocturnal palette — deep teal-navy shadows, warm
amber pools around the buildings, a big low moon — painterly storybook realism,
rich texture, cohesive single-source moonlight, inviting and slightly magical.

## STYLE MODIFIERS (append if it needs steering)

painted storybook illustration, gouache and soft-pastel texture, warm cinematic
night lighting, cozy, hand-drawn cartography, game overworld map, cohesive
composition, high detail, atmospheric

## NEGATIVE / AVOID

text, letters, labels, words, signage typography, UI, HUD, watermark, people,
faces, cars, modern city, skyscrapers, harsh midday sun, flat satellite photo,
photorealistic drone shot, blurry, low-detail, oversaturated neon

## COMPOSITION NOTES (nudge the layout — loose is fine)

- **Keep the bottom-centre edge open** — the farm gate + mailbox sit there;
  that's where the buggy spawns and the first path starts.
- Church on a low rise toward the **upper area** (it becomes the 8-lantern
  heritage hill). Farmhouse roughly **upper-left**. Grain elevator standing
  **tall on one side**. Paths should actually connect the buildings, not just
  decorate — a driver needs routes.
- Leave a little breathing room at all four edges (the view vignettes/
  letterboxes on odd screens; nothing important in the corners).
